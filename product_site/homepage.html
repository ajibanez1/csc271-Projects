PantryPal

Description

Do you have ingredient that you arent using? or are hungry but don't know what to make? well that's where PantryPal comes into play. comes in! PantryPal is a web app where you are able to input multiple ingredients from your kitchen and have PantryPals give you high rated meals based on your ingredients

How to use

  1. Enter your Ingredients
  2. Press Search
  3. Select a meal
  4. And Start Cooking
